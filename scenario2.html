GPUs at a second site: clinical work stays home, only de-identified tensors travel

The hospital owns GPUs, but they sit at a second campus. Everything clinical stays at the main site exactly as in scenario 1. The gateway also de-identifies, crops and resamples each study, then stores and forwards one encrypted tensor to site 2 over a private link. Site 2 never sees patient identity and never reaches the PACS.

4AI triage through the gateway

  1. The PACS pushes the relevant seriesSame export rule as scenario 1, including earlier studies of the same patient when the AI needs them.
    DICOM C-STORE export
  2. The gateway de-identifiesPatient identity is removed from the tags, and instances that may carry text burned into the pixels (localizers, Secondary Captures, anything flagged with Burned In Annotation) are rejected; pixel redaction can be added later as a plugin step, as described in scenario 1. If the gateway cannot do this safely, it blocks the study: the study gets "no AI result" and nothing leaves the site.
  3. The gateway prepares one tensorIt crops and resamples the volume to exactly what the model needs, using the same steps as in training, and encrypts the result.
  4. Store and forward over N8The tensor is kept on the gateway until site 2 confirms receipt, so a link outage shorter than the deadline does not lose work. Once the deadline passes, the job is dropped and the tensor deleted.
    gRPC over mTLS · N8
  5. Site 2 runs the modelThe inference service returns a mask. It never sees who the patient is.
  6. The gateway maps the result backThe mask is placed back onto the original study, then sent to the PACS as DICOM SEG + SR by C-STORE. The priority flag goes to HIS/RIS.
    C-STORE · HL7 ORU / FHIR
Why one tensor and not slice by slice. The model reads the 3D volume in overlapping windows, in no fixed order. Fetching slices on demand over the link would multiply the number of round trips by hundreds; one tensor needs a single round trip.

5Site 2 and the N8 link

Site 2

GPUs only

Runs GPU inference, model training and the model registry. Holds no identifiable data, has no route to the PACS, the archive database or the identity key table.

N8

Private link

Leased line or private WAN, round trip 2 to 35 ms, mutually authenticated TLS. Carries only encrypted tensors and masks, labeled de-identified training data, and validated models coming back.

Internet

Not involved

N8 does not go through the egress firewall. An internet outage affects only the cloud copy, never the AI.

6Failure behaviour

FailureEffect
Site 2 or N8 is downEvery call has a timeout and a circuit breaker. When the deadline passes (120 s, or 90 s for emergency cases) the study shows "no AI result" and is read in normal order. The job is dropped: it is not sent when the link returns, and no late result or alert ever arrives. The clinical path is not affected.
Gateway cannot de-identify a studyThe study is blocked and shows "no AI result". Nothing leaves the site.
Gateway node failsThe load balancer stops sending to it and the other nodes carry on. Files and index are shared, so nothing is lost; dcm4chee retries any export that was interrupted.
Internet is downNo effect on the AI. Only the cloud copy waits.

The viewer always distinguishes three states: AI not run yet, AI failed or timed out ("no AI result"), and AI done and waiting for confirmation.

7High availability

PACS, PACS state and the backup path are as in scenario 1. The gateway runs as N+1 active-active nodes behind a load balancer, sharing one PostgreSQL index and keeping its files (including tensors waiting to be forwarded) in SeaweedFS, so any node can pick up a job and a node failure loses nothing.
